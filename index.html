Web app · AI

WhatsApp AI Assistant + CRM Panel

An assistant that answers customer messages on WhatsApp with AI and gathers every conversation in one web panel. The business owner sets the tone and the business details from the panel, and can step into any conversation.

When the assistant doesn't know the answer, it hands the conversation over to a person and stays quiet. The message flow is covered by an end-to-end integration test that mocks the WhatsApp provider.

Online store

DE L'AMOUR

A live online store for a laundry fragrance brand, built so the brand can sell directly instead of through a marketplace. Customers browse the catalogue, order as a guest or a member, and follow their order with a tracking number.

The seller panel covers products, stock, categories, orders and shipping, with separate roles for the owner and for sellers. Access is enforced by database security rules, which are covered by automated tests together with the storefront flows.

  • Firebase
